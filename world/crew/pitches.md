# Crew pitches (crew spec §1): what a hire says on the HIRE card. ## <specialty> <GRADE>, then lines.

## ears GREEN
- "Nobody looks at the cleaner. I clean everywhere."
- "I sit at the bar and drink one beer all night. People forget I'm there."

## ears HAND
- "I hear things. Some of them are even true. I'll tell you which."
- "Bartenders know everything. I pour."

## ears PRO
- "Half the Strand tells me their troubles. The other half tells me theirs."
- "I can tell you who's sleeping where, and who's lying about it."

## ears VET
- "Forty years of listening. I know what a room sounds like before it goes wrong."
- "I don't ask questions. Asking is how you get noticed."

## face GREEN
- "I sell insurance. Mostly to people who didn't know they needed it."
- "Put me in a nice coat and I'll be anyone you need."

## face HAND
- "Doors open for me. I'm not sure why. I don't ask."
- "I can talk a Sentinel out of a ticket and into an apology."

## face PRO
- "I've been three different people this month. All of them paid."
- "Tell me who you need me to be by eight. I'll be them by nine."

## face VET
- "I've lied to better men than you, and they thanked me for it."
- "Every story's true if you tell it right. I tell it right."

## fence GREEN
- "I know what it's worth. You don't. That's why you need me."
- "I've got a stall and a cousin with a van. That's a start."

## fence HAND
- "Bring it in hot. I'll cool it."
- "I've got buyers for chips, decks, paper. People, if you're desperate. I'm not."

## fence PRO
- "Everything has a price. I find it, and then I find someone who'll pay twice."
- "The night market has four rules. I wrote two of them."

## fence VET
- "I moved goods for the Counting House before Mother Rot. I'll be moving them after."
- "Don't haggle with me. I've already done it for both of us."

## runner GREEN
- "I've been inside buildings I've never seen."
- "Old deck, new tricks. Mostly old tricks."

## runner HAND
- "Give me a terminal and ten minutes. You'll have the schedules."
- "I loop cameras. I pull records. I don't ask what for."

## runner PRO
- "I was almost somebody, once. Now I'm the somebody you call."
- "Every system in this city has a back door. I keep a list."

## runner VET
- "I jacked into the old grid before they buried it. I know where the bodies are."
- "I'm slow now. I'm also never wrong."

## muscle GREEN
- "I'm big. I show up. I don't talk much. What else do you need?"
- "I worked doors on the Strand. Nobody got past who wasn't supposed to."

## muscle HAND
- "I collect. People pay. That's the whole job."
- "I've been hit harder than you'd think. Still standing."

## muscle PRO
- "I don't start fights. I end them. Then I go home."
- "Debtors see me and find the money. I don't even have to knock."

## muscle VET
- "I've stood behind worse than you. I've stood in front of worse, too."
- "My hands are bad now. My reputation does the work."
