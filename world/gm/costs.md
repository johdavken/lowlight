# The GM's written lines (roll lifecycle spec §1.4)

A dealt cost shows one of these until the GM's own line arrives, and keeps it if the GM's never comes. One terse line each, second person, no game words.

# alarm
- Somewhere a log fills in your name.
- A phone rings once in a back office, and someone answers it.
- A camera you missed turns, slow, and holds.
- Down the hall a radio asks a question nobody answers yet.

# heat
- Someone across the street has your face now, and a reason to sell it.
- A kid on a scooter watches you a beat too long, then rides off fast.
- Your name goes into somebody's notebook tonight.

# worse
- The way ahead isn't empty any more.
- Someone has stopped what they were doing up ahead, and is listening.
- A second lock clicks shut where you're going next.

# small
- The window you were counting on is closing, and you can feel it.
- Whatever was waiting ahead has already been picked over.
- The next door opens onto less than you hoped.

# take
- Some of what you carry slips away into the rain.
- A seam gives, and part of what you carried is gone before you notice.
- You leave a piece of it behind; there's no going back for it.
