## On his mind
The Waterkeepers still send your crate back, and for once you have something they need.

## Giver knows
Word came up the canals that a surge cracked a main gate valve at one of the Drownworks pump houses, and the corps are slow-walking the replacement, the way they do when they want the Works to come asking. Your divers have pulled exactly that kind of valve out of a drowned plant under the Marsh, sound, and you have it in the Counting House. The Waterkeepers won't deal with you. But someone who isn't you could carry it to them. You'd tell someone who's already done you a kindness, or someone who asks about the Works, and you'd say you want nothing for it. That isn't quite true.

## Receiver knows
A surge cracked the main gate valve on Pump House Nine's second pump weeks ago. Your crew has been nursing it with clamps and prayers, and the corps keep "processing the order". It's your crew's safety on the line every night. If someone offers you a part, the first thing you'll want to know is where it came from, and if it came from the Marsh, from him, you'll be angry, and then tempted, and you won't like either.
