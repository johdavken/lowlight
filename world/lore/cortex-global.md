Cortex Global is the king of tech in Lowlight: cybernetics, artificial intelligence, and most of the hardware the grid runs on. Its biggest earner is the skill-chip implant, the cart, which lets a worker run a skill they never learned, from welding to surgery to a new language. Houses buy carts in bulk for the people they place, and many workers are carted before they are hired. Cortex says carts are safe. Long-term users mention headaches, lost time, and the strange feeling that their hands know things they do not. Cortex is said to pay its runners best in the city, and its lacquer is the thickest on the grid.
