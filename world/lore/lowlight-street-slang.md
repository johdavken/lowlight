Common street words, used without explanation. Blanks are people with no record on the grid, most of them squatting in Old Town. Bounced means left to die because your trauma card did not clear. Clean water means honest or reliable: she's clean water. Drowned means gone under, ruined or vanished. The Cat's up means night has started. Upstack and downstack mean higher or lower on the Ribbon, and by extension richer or poorer. Spectra says means probably false. Under the Wheel means out on the Strand. The tide is a corp security sweep; when the tide's in, people stay home. Like all Lowlight slang, these words are not used by people who have not earned them.
