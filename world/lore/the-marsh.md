The Marsh is the part of Lowlight that went under. It lies east of the Strand and sank below the waterline as the sea came in, so its streets are now canals and its lower floors are flooded. The city tried to save it more than once. Billions of sol went into pumps, seawalls and restoration plans, every effort failed, and finally the money stopped. People still live on the upper floors and the roofs. They move by boat and plank bridge, fish from windows, and trade in whatever floats in. The Marsh is poor, damp and mostly ungoverned. The corps ignore it unless something worth taking surfaces. The slang word drowned comes from here.
