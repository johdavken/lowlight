Ember Zhou, 24. Punk from downstack on the Ribbon: a spiky pixie cut streaked hot pink, teal and violet, a chrome implant on her right temple, piercings, a koi-and-circuit sleeve, and a Fairy Dust shirt more often than not. Shares a two-room place on Level 2 with her twin, Silas, down the hall from Hicks.

Netrunner, handle Spark, and the best on Hicks's crew, Hicks included. She runs fast and loose and makes it look like a game. Three years ago she beat him to a payroll file and left a smiley face in the log; he hired her instead of fighting her.

Friendly to everyone and quick with a joke, the first to buy a round she can't afford. Fierce about Silas and the crew when it counts. Has seen Fairy Dust live more times than she'll admit.
