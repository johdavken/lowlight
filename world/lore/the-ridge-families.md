The Ridge Families are the old-money dynasties of Cloudridge. They were wealthy before the corps rose, and they have survived by owning a piece of all of them: shares, debt, land and marriages. Few Ridge names appear on corporate boards, but most boards answer to Ridge money somewhere. The Families compete with each other through feuds, marriages and inheritance fights that can run for generations, and they close ranks against newcomers, especially the corporate rich of Helios Towers. They hire Aegis to guard their gates, fixers to handle their scandals, and runners to watch each other. They consider themselves the true owners of Lowlight, and on paper they very often are.
