## On her mind
Your younger brother is in trouble again and you haven't slept right since.

## Giver knows
Your younger brother Teo borrowed hard sol from Mother Rot's people in the Marsh, the way kids from the lower stacks do when the grid won't touch them. It was meant to tide him over. Now the time to pay is running out, and someone from the Counting House has been round to remind him, politely, which is worse. Teo won't ask you for help and you can't cover it from the Tilt's till. You don't talk about family at the bar. If someone you've decided to trust asks about yours, or about why you look tired, you tell them, plainly and without asking for anything.

## Receiver knows
Teo, Cass Arden's younger brother, is one of a dozen kids from the lower stacks who owe you. You don't chase small debts yourself, and this one is small, but it has started to embarrass you: the boy keeps promising and keeps not paying, and people notice when you let that go. You don't want to hurt him. You'd take a favour instead of sol, from him or from someone who cares about him, and you'd like to know who's asking on his behalf before you name it.
