The Drowned Choir is a cult in the Marsh that believes the sea is judgment. It teaches that Lowlight was condemned for its greed, that the Marsh was only the first to fall, and that the water will keep rising until it takes Nexus, the Towers and the Shelf. Members gather on rooftops and in half-flooded halls to sing at the waterline, and they baptize converts in the canals. As far as anyone can prove, they are not violent, but people who wrong them have a habit of drowning, and Choir members talk about it calmly. The Choir takes in the desperate and gives them food and purpose, which makes it popular in the Marsh and spreading into the Spine. The Sump-Rats trade with them and keep their distance.
