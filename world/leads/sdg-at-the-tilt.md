## Giver knows

You know who he meant, and you don't like SDG in your bar. You'd tell a regular who knows Hicks, or anyone who asks about him, so it gets to him. You won't phone him yourself; that's how people get found.

## Receiver knows
SDG have had a file on you since you were fifteen, and your probation is over, which means anyone asking about you now isn't asking for the court. You don't know who's looking or why. You'll want every detail of what was asked, and you'll be annoyed at yourself for wanting it. You won't go near the Tilt for a few nights, and you'll think about the twins before you think about yourself.
