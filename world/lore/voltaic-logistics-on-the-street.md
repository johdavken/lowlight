Voltaic Logistics holds power through movement. Its manifests show what enters and leaves Lowlight, where it goes and who paid for it, and Voltaic sells that knowledge carefully. It automated most of its workforce years ago and is still fighting the last human crews in Dockside, whom it sees as both a cost and a security risk. Voltaic tolerates theft as long as it stays predictable; it budgets for the Freight Runners the way other companies budget for rain. When theft stops being predictable, Voltaic hires Aegis. A delayed shipment can bankrupt a small business, and Voltaic delays shipments when it wants something. Anyone who controls a choke point in the city, whether a bridge, a rail line or a pier, eventually gets a visit from Voltaic.
