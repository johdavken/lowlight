Titan is a corp in slow collapse, which makes it dangerous. Its mining fleets are gone and its import business shrinks every year, but it still owns land, warehouses, old debts and a great deal of hard sol. Titan's board is at war with itself. Different factions inside the company hire different crews, runners and fixers, sometimes against each other. Titan pays better than anyone for risky work because it has to: few people want to be tied to a company that may not exist next year. It is rumored to be quietly selling off assets, including orbital hardware nobody knew was still up there. Everyone in Nexus is waiting to see who picks over Titan's carcass, and Titan has no intention of dying quietly.
