A deaf Meridian is a Cortex deck with its check-in chip pulled, so it no longer reports to Cortex. It is nearly as fast as a clean one and far cheaper, because most of them are stolen. Second Socket sells them under the counter and doesn't ask. The catch is the serial number, etched into the board where it can't be filed off without killing the deck. If a Caper or Cortex ever reads it, the person holding it is the person who bought a stolen leash. Runners use one for a year or two, then sell it on before it gets famous.
