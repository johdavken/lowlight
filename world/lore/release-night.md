Release night is the night a house lets go of its workers' pay. Every house sets its own schedule and can change it, so the Ribbon's bars, stalls and lenders all keep a list of who releases when. For one night a whole house has money. They fill the same bars and eat something with a real egg in it. Tabs come due at the door, and what's owed between friends gets paid in front of witnesses. Scoma's dealers drop their prices and the recruiters from other houses buy rounds. By morning most of it has gone to rent, the script and the people who were waiting for it. A house that holds back a release can empty a street for a week. A house that does it twice finds out what its workers are carted for.
