Helios Towers, called the Towers, is where Lowlight's corporate elite live, east of Cloudridge. There are only five towers, but each one is effectively its own city, with its own housing, schools, clinics, shops, security and rules. Each is governed separately, and city law applies inside only as far as each tower allows. Residents can go years without touching the ground. Workers from below come in through service entrances, are scanned on the way in and out, and are not allowed above certain floors. From the street, the Towers look like the future Lowlight was promised. From inside, the city below looks like weather.
