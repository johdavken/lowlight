The Capers are runners who hunt runners. They work for whoever pays: Cortex, Spectra, MiraGen, SDG, the Shelf. They trace intrusions to their source, burn decks remotely, and sell names and locations to the people who want them. Capers are well paid and well protected. Many were once Strip-Miners, or worse, who got caught and were offered a choice. The rest of the runner world despises them. Capers rarely make the physical arrest themselves; they find the person, and SDG or Aegis does the rest. Runners say the name is a joke about the jobs they ruin, and every runner in Lowlight has a story about one.
