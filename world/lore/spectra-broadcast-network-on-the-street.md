Spectra rules by narrative. Its Nexus newsrooms take commissions from corporate boards, the Shelf and anyone else who can pay, so the story of the day is whatever was bought that morning. Its archive division rewrites old broadcasts, and its runners hunt down surviving copies of the originals. Spectra's anchors are celebrities, and its street crews film anything violent enough to sell. Spectra can make a person famous, guilty or forgotten overnight. Its two great enemies are True-North, who preserve the originals, and Dead Air, who jam its feeds; on air, Spectra calls both of them terrorists. Most people in Lowlight don't believe Spectra, but they watch it anyway, and whatever Spectra says becomes the official record whether anyone believes it or not.
