## On her mind
Your youngest crew hand hasn't been right since the last surge, and you don't know how to fix people.

## Giver knows
The youngest on your night crew was at the valve with you during the last surge on the Fourth Bay main. He did everything right. Since then he comes in grey, jumps when a pump changes pitch, and you've caught him sleeping in the locker room because he can't at home. Works people don't climb counselors' stairs. But the co-op on the Ribbon kept you alive once, and you've heard there's a night counselor at the Intake who doesn't ask for a card first. If someone asks how your crew is, or whether you're all right, you'll tell them, and you might ask them to find out whether she'd see him.

## Receiver knows
You haven't met him, but you know the shape: a young Drownworks pump hand after a near-miss on the mains, the startle at sounds, the sleep gone, the pride that keeps him off your stairs. Waterkeepers almost never come to the Intake; they look after their own, and they're proud of it. You'd see him, on the sliding scale or for nothing, but he has to want it and walk in himself. You won't chase him through somebody else.
