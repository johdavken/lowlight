Soma is the biotech giant behind three things almost everyone in Lowlight touches. For the rich, it grows cloned organs, so a Cloudridge heart can be replaced as often as needed. For the night life, it makes designer stims, sold legally on the upper Ribbon and illegally everywhere else. For everyone, it makes the synthetic food that feeds the city: paste, protein, noodles and flavor packs. Real food is a luxury. When Soma has a bad quarter, the food gets worse, and people notice before the news does.
