The grid is Lowlight's network, and almost everything that matters happens there first. It looks old: amber and green text, scan lines, sound through cheap speakers. Runners connect through decks, which are heavy keyboard units with cartridge slots and a cable to a jack behind the ear. They do the work nobody admits to paying for: moving money, erasing records, stealing designs, burying stories before Spectra can run them. Every corp employs runners and every corp hunts them. Most people never know a runner has touched their life. They only know that a debt vanished, a file appeared, or a door stopped opening. Ordinary people treat the grid like weather. They use it but do not trust it, and anything they cannot afford to lose goes on paper.
