The Waterkeepers are the crews of Drownworks, organized into a tight brotherhood that runs the city's water reclamation. Officially they are a workers' guild. In practice they are one of the most respected and most dangerous groups in Lowlight, because they control something every person in the city needs every day. They guard the settling bays, pump houses and mains themselves, and they treat any threat to the water as an act of war. They take no bribes, make no deals with Scoma or the Sump-Rats, and keep the corps at arm's length. The Waterkeepers have gone on strike only twice. Both times the city stopped, and the corps gave in within days. Being called clean water by a Waterkeeper is high praise.
