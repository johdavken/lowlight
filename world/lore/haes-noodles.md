Hae's Noodles is a street stall on Level 4 of the Ribbon, directly under Lowlight Intake: a deep wooden counter, a handful of worn stools, a striped dark-red awning with paper lanterns under it, and a red neon bowl-and-chopsticks sign with no words on it, because everyone on the level knows what it is. An old vending machine hums beside it. The stockpot is never off. Hae serves one broth and two noodles, with whatever topping was cheap at the market that morning. Like most food on the Ribbon, it's mostly Soma, but the broth is her own, and it tastes like it. She takes hard sol, runs a tab for people she trusts, and doesn't discuss either. Hae says little and notices everything, and she doesn't gossip about anyone who eats at her counter. Regulars don't order; she already knows. Half the Intake's clients eat here before they go up the stairs, and some never go up at all, and she lets them sit as long as they need. When the late ones come down from upstairs, a second bowl is already out.
