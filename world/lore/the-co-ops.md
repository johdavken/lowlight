The co-ops are Lowlight's mutual aid network: sliding-scale clinics, counseling offices, food kitchens, legal help and shelters, run by volunteers and paid for with whatever comes in. Lowlight Intake on the Ribbon is one of them. The co-ops are the only safety net most people in the city have, and they serve anyone who walks in, from blanks to bounced workers to off-script chrome. They keep their records on paper and off the grid, and they will not tell SDG who came through the door. They have no weapons and little money, but they have many quiet friends: the Waterkeepers, the Copper-Heads, Dead Air, and plenty of people who once needed help themselves. The corps find them inconvenient. The gangs mostly leave them alone, because sooner or later everyone needs a co-op.
