## On her mind
Silas has been jacked in every night this week and he won't tell you what he's chasing.

## Giver knows
Your twin has gone deep on the grid as Gumshoe before, but this week is different: every night, till dawn, and he shuts his deck when you walk in. Yesterday you saw a Spectra watermark on his screen and an old date. He's eating standing up and talking to himself. You're scared he's poking at something Spectra flagged, and that they'll find him. You'd tell anyone who asks about Silas, or why you look worried, and you'd want them to get him talking, because he won't talk to you about it.

## Receiver knows
You found raw footage from the second water strike, years old, from before Spectra cut it into the version everyone remembers. The city is dark in every frame, except one pump house in Drownworks, lit, with water going out on a line nobody voted for, toward the co-op clinics on the Ribbon. Spectra's version leaves that out. You don't know what it means yet, and you haven't told Ember because she'd worry. If someone raises it, you'll be relieved to talk, a little too fast, and you'll want to know whether anyone ever asked the Waterkeepers.
