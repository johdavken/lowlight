## On his mind
A pump house that was lit when the whole city was dark, and nobody ever asked why.

## Giver knows
In the uncut footage of the second strike, one Drownworks pump house is running when every other light in the city is out, and the flow runs toward the co-op clinics. Spectra cut it. You've matched the building's windows and the bay behind it as best you can, and you want a Waterkeeper to tell you you're wrong, or right. You'd tell someone who asks what you found, or who mentions the Works, and you'd ask them to ask, because you'd stammer.

## Receiver knows
Someone has old footage of the second strike, and it shows a pump house running when the line was holding. You know exactly which one and exactly whose hand opened the bypass. If it's raised, you'll go very still, ask what they saw and who else has it, and give nothing away about yourself. You'll want the footage kept quiet, for the clinics' sake as much as yours. Whether you ever say more depends on whether this person has proved they're clean water.
