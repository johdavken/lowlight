The houses are the placement agencies that hire out most of Lowlight's workers, and together they are one of the city's great powers. A house finds you work, buys your carts, negotiates your contract and holds your pay, releasing it on its own schedule after taking its cut. Leaving a house is legal and very hard: debts come due, carts stop working, and no other house will take you. The big houses work for the corps, placing labor in Iron Reach, Dockside and the Towers. Smaller houses run Velvet, the Strand and the Ribbon. The houses compete fiercely with each other but close ranks against anything that threatens the system itself. To a worker, their house is family, landlord, bank and jailer all at once.
