The Breakers rule the habs beneath the Spine. They began as a crew that broke into maintenance shafts and service decks, and they now control the inside of the highway: its stairwells, cable tunnels, and the hidden routes between pylons. They tax anything that moves through their territory, tap power from the Spine's lighting lines, and sell freight that falls, or is pushed, from the decks above. Breakers mark territory by smashing the lights; when a stretch under the Spine goes dark, it belongs to them. They are loud, young and violent, and their fights sometimes spill up onto the Spine itself. The Spiners who live alongside them pay their dues and keep their doors locked.
