SDG's headquarters is the Sentinel Tower in Nexus, a dark glass column with no logo on it, because everyone already knows. The public sees the lobby: grey stone, a scanner arch, and a desk where people who have been called in wait to be told why. The sixty-first floor has no number on the lift panel. It is the branch's floor. Carpet that swallows footsteps, glass walls between the offices, real wood on the desks, a coffee machine that costs more than a year in a Spine hab, and windows from floor to ceiling. Rain runs down them most nights, and the city below looks like weather. Nothing on the floor is loud. Nobody raises their voice, nobody runs, and nobody looks at another officer's screen. Case officers come up when they are called and go back down when they are done. It is the cleanest room most of them will ever stand in, and none of them stay longer than they have to.
