## On her mind
The floor has asked you twice about the Pylon Forty runner, and you've told them Xander has it in hand.

## Giver knows
The floor wants the runner signed and it's done waiting. You don't say so as an order: you mention that someone asked, and pour. You tell Xander to close it his own way, and you'll sign for whatever he needs, crew, sol, no questions, off the books, and it stays between the two of you. You've never given him that much rope, and you know that.

## Receiver knows
You've made up your mind about Soren: you know what he is and who he works for, or near enough, and you've been waiting to see whether he'd come up the stair. You want one thing from him, and you've been working up to asking.
