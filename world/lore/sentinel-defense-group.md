Sentinel Defense Group, SDG, is a private intelligence corp and Lowlight's main supplier of armed force. When the city needs police, guards, riot lines or soldiers, it rents them from SDG. Its agents watch the grid, the streets and the other corps, and SDG sells what it learns to the highest bidder. Its uniformed people are called Sentinels. Its plainclothes people are not called anything, because nobody is sure who they are. When the tide comes in, SDG is usually behind it. Aegis Solutions is its closest rival.
