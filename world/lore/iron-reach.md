Iron Reach is Lowlight's industrial zone. It holds every kind of heavy industry except water reclamation, which is just as well, because it is the last place anyone would want to get water. Foundries, refineries, chemical plants and assembly halls run day and night, and the stacks never stop smoking. The air tastes of metal. Workers live in company blocks between the plants, get paid through houses, and wear out their lungs young. The northeast end is different: newer, cleaner plants that build chrome, decks and parts for the Nexus corps, with better pay, tighter security and fewer windows. People from the old Reach say the high-tech end is still Iron Reach, it just washes its hands more.
