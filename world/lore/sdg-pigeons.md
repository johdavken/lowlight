Pigeons is what the street calls Sentinels on patrol, the ones in the grey. The name started downstack and went everywhere. Pigeons are grey, they are on every corner where somebody feeds them, and they are never on a corner where nobody does: Nexus, the Shelf, the Towers' service levels, any block whose owners pay for coverage. Downstack, the only grey anyone sees comes in with a tide. Pigeons walk in pairs, write everything down and move on when the shift ends. Most are underpaid, bored and a long way from the money SDG makes on them, and the smart ones can be rented by the hour. The uniform carries the Sentinel eye and nothing else. Patrol has no bird of its own inside SDG, and Sentinels hate the one the street gave them. Saying it to one's face costs a fine. Saying it where they can't prove who said it is a Ribbon pastime.
