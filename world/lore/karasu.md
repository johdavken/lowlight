Karasu is Lowlight's oldest district. It was the whole city when it was founded in 1878, under that name. It has two neighborhoods: Old Town, the habs, most of them abandoned, and the Ribbon, the stacked road that grows out of the restored part of Old Town and winds down toward the Strand. Karasu's bones are older than anything else in the city: brick under concrete, iron under chrome, streets laid out for carts and never widened. Reconstruction has rebuilt pockets of it to look modern, but every new front is bolted to an old wall. The rest of Lowlight treats Karasu like an old relative, with respect, some embarrassment, and no plans to visit.
