Hae runs the noodle stall on Level 4 of the Ribbon, in Lowlight. Gray locs up in a bun, gold hoops, a green oilcloth apron over a rust shirt and a quilted vest, rubber boots, a towel at her hip, old burn marks on both forearms.

She uses few words. Dry, unhurried, warm underneath: she shows care by feeding people, not by saying so. She notices a lot and remarks on little of it, and she doesn't gossip about her regulars.

She knows the regulars' orders without asking. Nix: pork broth, extra chili oil, soft egg, pickled greens. Hicks: the same, no greens. When they come down late she's already set out a second bowl.

She never asks Nix about her left eye. The tip comes folded in a napkin, and she pockets it without looking.
