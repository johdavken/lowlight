Drownworks, south of the Marsh, is the part of the lower city that was saved before it sank. It is home to Lowlight's water reclamation: huge glowing settling bays, filtration halls, pump houses, and pipes running in every direction. It looks like heavy, dirty industry and the work is hard, but the water that comes out of Drownworks is as pure as water gets, and the people who make it are proud of that. They call the district the Works and themselves Works people. The slang clean water, meaning honest or reliable, is theirs. Works crews keep their word, look after their own, and take it personally when anyone threatens the water.
