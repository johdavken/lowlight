Velvet is Lowlight's red-light quarter, north of the Strand at the foot of the cliffs of Mirage Heights. Clubs, lounges, bathhouses and rooms rented by the hour fill its streets, and most of the workers are placed by houses that take a cut and hold their pay. On the cliff face above it sits a giant neon cat, visible across the city and plainly from Cloudridge. The Cat lights at dusk, which is why people say the Cat's up when night begins. The Cat glows directly beneath the Shelf, so Lowlight's government works every day above the brightest sign in the red-light quarter, and the city finds this very funny. Velvet is cleaner and quieter than outsiders expect. What it sells most is discretion.
