The Switchboard is the loose network of fixers who connect Lowlight's factions. A Switchboard fixer can find a runner, a crew, a smuggler, a doctor or a buyer for almost anything, and takes a cut for the introduction. There is no leader and no headquarters, only a web of people who vouch for each other, pass work along, and keep a strict code: never work both sides of the same job, never name a client, never sell out another fixer. Anyone who breaks the code is cut off, which in Lowlight is often fatal. The Switchboard deals with corps, gangs and clans alike and belongs to none of them. Most of the work done in the city's shadows passes through it at some point.
