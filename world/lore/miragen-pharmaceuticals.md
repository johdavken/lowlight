MiraGen Pharmaceuticals makes combat drugs for SDG and Aegis, neural boosters for students and runners, and the gene-therapy edits that keep heavy chrome users from going into the hum, which the corps call cyber-psychosis. The edits are not a cure but a subscription, renewed monthly at a steep price, and Lowlight calls it the script. Anyone with enough chrome is on the script or knows they should be. Going off script means missing a payment, and people off script are avoided on sight. MiraGen says the subscription funds research. Everyone else says MiraGen found a way to rent out sanity.
