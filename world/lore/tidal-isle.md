Tidal Isle sits in the bay as the city's front door. Every ship and passenger arriving in Lowlight clears customs here, and every immigrant is processed here, in long halls of booths, scanners and waiting benches. Getting through can take an hour or a year, depending on papers, sol and luck. Some people never get past the Isle. They live in the holding blocks and work the piers while their cases sit on someone's screen. Smugglers, fixers and forgers all keep business on the Isle, because the easiest way into Lowlight is through the front door with someone holding it open. The northwest corner belongs to the QDF, and new arrivals learn quickly not to look at it for long.
