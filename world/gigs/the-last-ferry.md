Her husband ran cargo through the Deep for forty years and died owing half the docks. Her papers still carry his surname, and three different houses are watching booth nine for it. The Ferrymen will take her debt in labour, but only if she reaches them under a name nobody is collecting on. Change the name without leaving a trail back to the old one.
