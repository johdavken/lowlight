The OhhCee's, short for the overclocked, are heavily chromed people who went off script and decided not to come back. Some couldn't afford MiraGen's subscription; others refused it. They push their chrome past its limits and treat the hum as a high, a faith and a warning to everyone else. They are fast, strong and unpredictable, and they burn out young, so the membership is always changing. They hold no territory, only whatever place they are drinking or fighting in that night, usually on the lower Ribbon or the Strand. Other gangs hire them as shock troops and regret it. Ordinary people cross the street when they see one coming. MiraGen points to them as proof of what happens without the script, and Spectra puts them on the news whenever renewals come due.
