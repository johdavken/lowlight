Herons are SDG's custody wing: the officers who move prisoners, and the boats that carry them across to the Q on Tidal Isle. Unlike most of SDG's names, the street knows this one. Herons wear long grey coats, work in pairs and are patient. A heron doesn't break a door. It waits on the landing, or across the street, or at the end of the bar, until the person it came for comes out. Arrest by a Sentinel means a cell, a fee and, most of the time, a way back. Collection by a heron means the Q. "The heron's at the door" means someone is going across the water, and old families in Karasu and Dockside still leave a light in the window the night a heron takes one of theirs. Herons sign for every prisoner and for nothing that happens to them on the other side. What the Q does with people is not their department, and they will tell you so.
