A ripperdoc low on the Ribbon, in an old repair shop behind a steel shutter that never goes all the way up. There is no name on it, only a red neon open hand in the door's window, and that is what everyone calls it. Inside: one surgical chair with the straps left hanging, a ring lamp on a jointed arm, a cabinet of second-hand chrome (hands, eyes, spinal plates, coils of neural cable, most of it out of somebody else) and a fridge of MiraGen script sold at a markup to people who are a week from being off script. It is what Karasu gets instead of Chiron: nobody checks your card, the work is good, and nobody asks where the parts came from. It is clean around the chair and nowhere else, and it never closes all the way either.
