The sol is Lowlight's currency. The name is short for solutions per second, the measure of mining speed the money was first pegged to. Its value still rides on how much computing the city's banks can prove. The word is the same in singular and plural: one sol, a billion sol. Most sol moves as numbers on the grid, through accounts the corps can see. Hard sol, meaning printed scrip and coin, still circulates in Karasu, the Marsh and Velvet, and anyone who pays in hard sol is assumed to have a reason. Being broke is being sol-sick. Prices rise with altitude. The same bowl of noodles costs more on the upper Ribbon than the lower, and more again on the Shelf, if anyone there ate noodles.
