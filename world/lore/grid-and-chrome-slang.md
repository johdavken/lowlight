Words from the grid and the chrome trade. Lacquer is corp security on the grid, the layered defenses wrapped around anything worth taking; thick lacquer kills. The dark is the unlisted back half of the grid, where runners meet and nothing is indexed. Carts are skill-chip implants, named for the cartridge they slot in like. A carted worker runs a skill they never learned, and borrowed hands is the polite word for them. The hum is the noise chrome makes in the mind before the mind breaks; the corps call it cyber-psychosis. The script is the MiraGen subscription that keeps the hum down, and people who miss a payment are off script. A ghost is a runner nobody has ever seen in person, which describes most of the good ones.
