A chrome deck has no box. It is built into the runner, along the forearm or behind the eye, with the keyboard replaced by the nerves that used to drive the hand. It is the fastest way onto the grid and the one thing nobody can take off you at a door. It is also the heaviest chrome a body can carry, and it makes the hum louder than anything else: chrome-deck runners are the first to need the script and the first to go off it badly. Cortex fits them uptown. Ripperdocs fit cheaper ones downstack, and everyone knows which kind lasts.
