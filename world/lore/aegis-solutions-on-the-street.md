Aegis Solutions hires fast, trains little and pays on results. Its crews guard tower lobbies, Cloudridge gates, Dockside yards and Iron Reach plants, and they are known for shooting first and filing reports later. Aegis takes the jobs SDG thinks are beneath it, and the jobs SDG would refuse. It will guard anyone with sol, including people SDG is watching, which is how the two keep colliding. Many Aegis officers are ex-Sentinels who were pushed out, and they brought their old grudges and old contacts with them. Aegis loyalty lasts exactly as long as the contract. When the contract ends, a former client can become a target by morning. Street people find Aegis easier to bribe than SDG and much harder to predict.
