The tide is SDG's sweep teams, and inside SDG they are called cormorants. They come in black kit instead of the grey, in numbers, from the water side of a district, and they leave the same way with whoever they were paid to take. A tide is always bought. A corp wants a block cleared before a sale, the Shelf wants a crowd gone before a vote, a house wants a rival's runners off its street, and SDG sells the sweep by the hour. The name comes from the old fishing birds of Karasu, which wore a ring round the throat so they could catch but not swallow. Cormorants keep nothing they catch: the client gets the people, the files and the decks, and SDG gets the fee. On the street nobody says cormorant. They say the tide's in, and they go home. The few who know the word say it the way the teams do: a cormorant doesn't eat what it catches.
