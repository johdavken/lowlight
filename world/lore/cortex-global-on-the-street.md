Cortex Global's real power on the street is the cart. Cortex doesn't so much sell skills as lease them. Carts are licensed and check in over the grid, so Cortex can update them, throttle them or switch them off. Houses pay Cortex for every carted worker they place, and a worker who leaves their house often finds their carts stop working that week. Cortex keeps the largest private runner force in Lowlight and pays it well, and other runners measure themselves against its lacquer. It buys out small tech shops, hires their best people and closes the rest. Cracked and bootleg carts are its main enemy on the street, and Cortex pays the Capers heavily to hunt the runners who crack them. Cortex rarely uses violence directly. It prefers lawsuits, debt and quiet updates, and it usually gets what it wants.
