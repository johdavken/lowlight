The Quantum Detainment Facility, the QDF, is Lowlight's great prison, a hard dark mass on the northwest corner of Tidal Isle. Officially it holds the city's most dangerous offenders. Unofficially it holds anyone the corps or the Shelf want gone. Nobody outside agrees on what the quantum in its name means, and the people who might know do not talk. Everyone does know that it holds more prisoners than it seems to have room for, that visits are rarely granted, and that the few people who come out stay quiet afterward. Locals call it the Q. Nobody jokes about it twice.
