The Neon Strand, the Strand to everyone, is where the Ribbon comes down and levels out at the water. It is Lowlight's entertainment district and the first part of the city to go fully neon; the rest of Lowlight copied it. The fairgrounds sit on the boardwalk over the bay: rides, games, arcades, food carts and shooting galleries, all a little older and more dangerous than they look. Families come in the early evening, and after the Cat's up, the crowd changes. The Strand sells the feeling of a good time to people who cannot afford a real one. Pickpockets, dealers and recruiters for the houses work the boardwalk every night, and corp security leaves them alone as long as the lights stay on.
