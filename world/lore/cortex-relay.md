Cortex Relay is the phone network almost everyone in Lowlight uses. It is a Cortex Global subsidiary, cheap enough that the Houses hand out Relay decks with a placement, and its towers cover every district except the deepest Hollows and the drowned blocks of the Marsh. Everyone knows the Relay keeps every text and sells what it reads. Corp security buys it by the district, Spectra buys it for stories, and a fixer can buy one person's week for the price of a good dinner. So people text the Relay as if someone is reading: plain talk, nothing about work, no names that matter. Anything that does matter goes in person, through a burner bought that morning, or "on copper": paid to the Copper-Heads and carried on the old Karasu lines, which the Relay cannot see.
