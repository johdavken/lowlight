Scoma is the cartel behind the knockoff stims flooding the Ribbon and the Strand. The name is short for Soma scams: cheap copies of Soma's designer stims, cooked in Iron Reach and the Marsh, packaged to look like the real thing, and sold for a fraction of the price. Some batches are nearly as good as Soma's, and some kill. Scoma dealers work the boardwalk under Wheel-Men protection and the lower Ribbon on their own, and Scoma pays the Freight Runners to bring precursors in through Dockside. Soma condemns Scoma in public and tolerates it in private, since the knockoffs keep demand high and make the real product look safe. Scoma's bosses are businesspeople, not street fighters, and they settle disputes with price wars before they settle them with guns.
