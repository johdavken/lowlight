A dock slate is a Voltaic Logistics freight terminal: a ruggedised grey deck with a rubber keyboard, built to be dropped off cranes and rained on. Thousands of them work the docks and the Spine, and they go missing all the time. Out of the box a slate only reads manifests. Cracked, it is a cheap, ugly, nearly unbreakable starter deck, slow and short on slots, and nobody looks twice at a dock worker carrying one. Most runners in the Strand and Dockside started on a slate, and some never saw a reason to stop.
