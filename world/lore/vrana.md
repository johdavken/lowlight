Vrana is a grey river port across the water, all tenements, cranes and dry docks along a slow brown river. It built ships for a century, until the yards were automated and most of the welders and riveters were let go in a single season. Vranans have been coming to Lowlight ever since, most of them through Tidal Isle and many through the Ferrymen, because a Vranan trade card counts for little at customs. In Lowlight they are known as steady, blunt workers who fix things nobody else will touch, and who never quite stop talking about the yards. Older Vranans keep to Dockside and the lower Ribbon, where the cranes at least look familiar. The name means crow, which Karasu people find either funny or a good omen, depending on the night.
