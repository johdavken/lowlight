Dockside lies across Lowlight Bay from Karasu, between the water and Iron Reach. It is the city's freight port: miles of stacked containers, piers and warehouses, with the big red cranes working through the night, nearly all of them Voltaic's. Most of the loading is automated now. The human crews that remain are hard, underpaid and protective of their jobs. The container stacks form a maze that changes every day, and plenty goes missing in it, some of it on purpose. Anyone who needs cargo moved quietly into or out of Lowlight ends up dealing with someone in Dockside.
