Liar's dice is the Ribbon's bar game. Everyone shakes under a cup, looks at their own dice and bids on what's under all of them, until someone calls liar and the cups come up. You need nothing but dice and something to hide them with, so it turns up on bar tops, hauler dashboards and the floor of the holding blocks. It is played for rounds, for sol, for a shift swapped, and on late nights for a truth, where the loser answers one question and has to mean it. People who would never tell you their real name will stake it on a pair of fours. Good players are said to read faces better than the Sentinels do, and most of them are lying about that too.
