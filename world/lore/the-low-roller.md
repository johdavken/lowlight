The Low Roller is the giant ferris wheel on the Strand's boardwalk. It is large enough to see from almost anywhere in Lowlight, its ring of lights turning slowly over the bay. Everyone calls it the Wheel. Its cars hold small parties, and some are rented by the hour for meetings nobody wants overheard, since a car at the top is the only private room on the Strand. People use the Wheel to find their way home and to tell which way they are facing. When it stops turning, which has happened only a few times, the whole city notices.
