Aegis Solutions is Lowlight's second-largest supplier of armed force, close behind Sentinel Defense Group. SDG sells intelligence and city contracts; Aegis sells protection: guards for the Towers and Cloudridge, escort crews, site security in Dockside and Iron Reach. It is cheaper than SDG and less careful. The two compete for every contract, and now and then their crews meet in the street, and Spectra reports it as an accident. Many Aegis people are ex-SDG, and they do not talk about why they left.
