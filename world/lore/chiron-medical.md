Chiron Medical runs Lowlight's high-end trauma hospitals and the ambulance drones that serve them. The care inside is excellent; getting inside depends on a trauma insurance card. When someone goes down, a drone scans for a card first, and if the card bounces, the drone flies on. People have bled out in the street with a Chiron drone hovering overhead, waiting to confirm. That is where the slang bounced comes from. Most of Lowlight relies on street clinics, back-room doctors and co-ops instead, and Chiron is happy to let them.
