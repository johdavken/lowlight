Lowlight has no room for its dead. Ground belongs to Cloudridge, where the Families keep walled plots, the last graves in the city with grass on them. Everyone else rents. Ashes go in a niche, in the old walls of Karasu or the feet of the pylons, leased by the year, and when the payments stop the niche is emptied into the bay. Before any of that the chrome comes out. Whoever holds the paper on it has the right to collect, a house or a lender or Cortex for its carts, and the collectors usually reach a body before the family does. That is why the second-hand cabinets on the Ribbon stay full. The Marsh gives its dead to the water from a boat. Nobody has ever counted the dead in the Hollows. On the Ribbon the wake is a tab left open at the dead person's bar. It stays open until the money runs out, and people judge a life by how long that takes.
