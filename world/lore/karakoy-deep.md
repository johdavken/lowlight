Karaköy Deep is a sea-gate city across the water, built on both banks of a deep strait that half the sea's shipping has to pass through. Its ferries run all night, its pilots know every current, and its docks have always been friendly to cargo that doesn't want to be counted. That reputation follows its people: customs on Tidal Isle look twice at anyone from the Deep, and the Ferrymen are said to hire its pilots for their night boats. Karaköy people in Lowlight work the water, the freight yards and the markets, trade in everything and keep their word once they have given it. They call the strait the Deep, and Lowlight has picked up the habit.
