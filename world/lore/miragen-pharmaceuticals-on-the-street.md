MiraGen's power is the script. Hundreds of thousands of chromed people in Lowlight need its monthly edits to keep the hum away, and MiraGen sets the price, the renewal date and the terms. Prices rise without warning, and accounts are suspended over billing errors. Subscribers who fall behind are offered payment plans that bind them to a house or a corp contract. MiraGen also supplies combat drugs to SDG and Aegis, and it tests new compounds in clinics in the poorer districts, paying volunteers in script. Its biggest threat is the Lazarus Net, and MiraGen spends heavily to find the Net's sellers and frighten its buyers. MiraGen likes to be seen as the company keeping Lowlight sane. The people on the script see it as the company keeping them on a leash.
