Old Town is Karasu's residential half, block after block of old habs. About three quarters of it is officially abandoned: no power on the meters, no names on the leases, windows bricked up or open to the rain. People live there anyway. They are the unknowns, blanks with no record on the grid, and they keep it that way. Lights move in buildings that have been dark for decades, and strangers who walk in without a reason do not always walk out. The rest of Old Town is reconstruction, blocks rebuilt with city money and corp grants, new skins on old frames. Those buildings look almost modern, and the people in them pay rent. At night they hear the dead blocks next door: water in the pipes, footsteps on stairs nobody should be using. The Ribbon starts in the reconstructed part and climbs from there.
