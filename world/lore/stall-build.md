A stall build is a deck put together downstack at a repair stall out of whatever came through: a salvaged board, mismatched keycaps, a screen from something else, extra cart slots soldered in where there was never meant to be room. Every one is different. The good ones beat a Meridian for slots and cost a quarter of the price, carry no serial anyone can trace, and run hot enough to burn a hand. They also die at bad moments. Runners say a stall build is only as good as the stall, and the stalls downstack on the Ribbon are where the good ones come from.
