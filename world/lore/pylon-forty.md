Pylon Forty is one of the Spine's great concrete legs, where the highway passes between Nexus and Karasu on its long run south toward the Marsh. Like most pylons it is a building now. Hab rings are cut into the concrete around the old maintenance lift, nine floors of them, and the higher you live, the closer you are to the road. The top ring sits right under the deck, where every heavy hauler hits an expansion joint with a sound like a door slamming, all night. Below the deck, between Forty and Forty-One, the hangers hang: habs built out of shipping steel and slung from the deck girders on cables, reached by a cable stair that sways when you use it. Their floors are grating, and under the grating is a long way down. At the foot of the pylon, open to the street under the deck, Oksana's counter keeps the lights on. A lift-maintenance office on the second ring has a company name on the door and nobody who ever fixes the lift. Spiners in Forty say you stop hearing the freight after a month, and you start hearing it again the night something is wrong.
