The Hollows are the blanks who hold Old Town's abandoned blocks. They are less a gang than a community with teeth: unregistered people, runaways, deserters and others the grid forgot, living in buildings the city wrote off. They guard the dead blocks against strangers, scavengers, SDG and reconstruction crews, and they have rigged the buildings with traps, false floors and blocked stairwells. Inside, they keep their own rules and their own peace. They distrust anyone with a grid record, which is almost everyone. Some Copper-Heads live among them and keep their lines running. The Hollows will sometimes trade with or help an outsider, but only through go-betweens, and never twice by the same route.
