On the street, SDG is the law, and it behaves like a business that sells it. Sentinels patrol Nexus, the Shelf, the Towers' service levels and anywhere else a client pays for coverage. Districts that don't pay only see them during a tide. Response time depends on who called. SDG keeps files on everyone who matters and plenty who don't, and it trades in favors as much as sol: a Sentinel can make a charge disappear, or appear. Its plainclothes branch, which officially has no name, runs informants on every level of the Ribbon. When it catches runners, it would rather buy them than jail them. SDG avoids open fights with gangs. It prefers to let them bleed each other and then arrest the survivors. Its rivalry with Aegis is the city's worst-kept secret. Crossing a single Sentinel is survivable. Crossing SDG as a company is not, and the people who try it end up in the Q.
