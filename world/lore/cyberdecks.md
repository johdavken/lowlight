A deck is how a runner gets on the grid: a heavy keyboard unit with cartridge slots, a screen of amber or green text, and a cable to the jack behind the ear. Decks differ in four ways runners care about: how fast they run, how many carts and programs they hold, how hot they are (how easily the deck leads back to whoever is using it), and, for chrome decks, how loud they make the hum. Corp decks are fast and traceable; street decks are slow, cheap and quiet; the rare ones are rare for a reason. New decks come from Cortex dealers uptown. Everything else comes from the Ribbon: Second Socket's counter and the room behind it, repair stalls downstack, and people who don't give their names. Nobody admits to owning more than one.
