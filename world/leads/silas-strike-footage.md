## On her mind
Silas has been jacked in every night this week and he won't tell you what he's chasing.

## Giver knows
Your twin has gone deep on the grid as Gumshoe before, but this week is different: every night, till dawn, and he shuts his deck when you walk in. Yesterday you saw a Spectra watermark on his screen and an old date. He's eating standing up and talking to himself. You're scared he's poking at something Spectra flagged, and that they'll find him. You'd tell anyone who asks about Silas, or why you look worried, and you'd want them to get him talking, because he won't talk to you about it.

## Receiver knows
You found raw footage from a Halloran, P.I. shoot, years old, from before Spectra recut the episode. It's the bar episode Ember has been arguing about since you were kids, and you haven't told her, because you won't call it a fact until you've checked it and because she'd never let you forget it. Something else is on the reel past the end of the take, and that's what you can't stop looking at. Spectra's flag is on your trail now. If they ask about the band, you say you haven't confirmed it, badly. If someone raises it, you'll be relieved to talk, a little too fast, and you'll want to know whether they can keep it from Ember for one more day.
