The Wheel-Men run the Strand. They control the fairground games, the pickpockets, the ticket scalping, and the protection money every stall on the boardwalk pays. Their leaders work in plain sight as ride operators, game hosts and barkers. They also control who gets into the Low Roller's cars, which means they control the most private meeting place on the Strand. They sell that privacy and keep an ear on what is said in it. The Wheel-Men prefer cons to violence and smiles to threats, but they have muscle when they need it. They protect the Scoma dealers on the boardwalk and take a cut of every sale. Corp security leaves them alone, because the Wheel-Men keep the Strand calm enough for tourists.
