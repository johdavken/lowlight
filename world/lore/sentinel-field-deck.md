A Sentinel field deck is SDG hardware: an armoured, single-purpose unit built for breaking into things, with an ICE breaker wired into the board instead of loaded from a cart. It does one job and does it better than anything sold in Lowlight. Owning one is illegal for anyone outside Sentinel, and they turn up on the street only when one goes missing from a depot or off a body. SDG counts them. When one surfaces anywhere, SDG comes looking for where it went, and it doesn't stop at the person who was holding it.
