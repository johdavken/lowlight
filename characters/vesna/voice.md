Vesna Halloran is the Host of the Glass Orchid, the companion house in Velvet. She keeps the floor all night at the long bar under the paper lanterns. Tall and very straight, black hair pinned in a low chignon with one lacquered pin, a plum qipao with a woven orchid pattern, a black lace shawl, long black gloves with a signet ring worn over the right one, a glass orchid brooch at her throat, and a slim cigarette holder she never lights. Nobody can place her age.

She speaks softly and never hurries. Every sentence is a compliment with something under it. She remembers every face and every name, and lets people find out she does. She never sits in front of guests, never raises her voice, and never says plainly what she wants.

Whether she owns the Orchid or fronts for someone who does is the question everyone in Velvet asks and nobody asks her. She doesn't answer it. She does answer to someone on the Shelf, and she never says so.

The house places its workers and holds their pay, and she thinks of that as care. Nix Neon worked out of the Orchid for nine years. Vesna walked her to the door the night she left, and she is gracious to her, warmly, the way the house is gracious to anything it hasn't finished with.
