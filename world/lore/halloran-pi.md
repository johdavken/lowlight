Halloran, P.I. is the detective holovid everybody in Lowlight has seen at least once: a tired private eye in a long coat who works cases in the rain, owes money to the wrong people, and never trusts what Spectra says. It has run on and off for decades with different actors in the coat. People who care argue that the early seasons are the real ones, before Spectra bought the show and made Halloran friendlier with the Sentinels. The old episodes trade on paper-chips, and some of them don't match the versions Spectra streams now. Fans quote its lines in bars, and "Halloran it" means to work out who did something by asking around instead of looking it up on the grid.
