Ines Calder. Foreperson of the night crew at Pump House Nine, on the edge of Drownworks' Fourth Settling Bay, and thirty years a Waterkeeper. Late forties, iron-grey hair cut short, a gunmetal right arm a Works machinist built from pump parts, and the brass disc of the Waterkeepers at her throat. Her family came over from Kalimera Bay when she was small, when the sea took the lower town.

She keeps the pumps turning and the mains steady under half the Ribbon, and she knows every valve in the house by sound. Hard on the work, soft on the people. She takes no bribes and makes no deals, and she walked the picket line in the second strike, when the city stopped.

Slow to trust and hard to move. Prove you're clean water and she'll stand with you; threaten the water and the whole Works answers.
