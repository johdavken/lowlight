Mirage Heights, called the Shelf, sits on the cliffs at the city's northeast edge, above Velvet. It holds Lowlight's government: council chambers, ministries, courts, and the homes of the officials who staff them, all in a guarded enclave with a view over the whole bay. Officially the Shelf governs Lowlight. In practice it signs what Nexus drafts and enforces it with security rented from SDG. Its officials are comfortable and well paid, and few of them have been down to the Ribbon in years. When something is decided on the Shelf, Lowlight assumes it was decided somewhere else first.
