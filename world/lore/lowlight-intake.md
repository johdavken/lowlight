Lowlight Intake is a sliding-scale counseling co-op in one room above Hae's Noodles on Level 4 of the Ribbon, up a flight of stairs off the street. It runs nights, 20:00 to 04:00, and Nix is its night counselor. Clients pay what they can, and the fee still has to clear. It sees ex-workers, Ribbon staff, dock burnouts, blanks and off-script chrome, and it refers out anything that needs a doctor. Like every co-op, it keeps its notes on paper, initials only, and it does not tell SDG who came through the door; the sign on the door says as much. The room does several jobs at once: a desk with three old screens and a lamp, a kitchenette with a kettle, a tall shelf of binders and files, and a daybed under a big window onto the Ribbon where people can sit, or sleep if they need to. Pipes and conduit run across every wall, the building is never fully quiet, and the smell of Hae's broth comes up through the floor all night.
