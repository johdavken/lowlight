Waterline Night is the one date Lowlight keeps for itself. It marks the night the city shut off the last of the Marsh pumps and stopped pretending. People chalk a line across their door at chest height, where the water stood on the Marsh's ground floors, and the rain has it gone by morning. In the Marsh the boats go out with lanterns through the drowned streets, the Sump-Rats take no fares, and names are read from the roofs until nobody has any left. The Drowned Choir sings at the waterline all night, and for once nobody minds. Up the Ribbon it is mostly an excuse to drink, though the bars leave one stool empty. No corp sponsors it and the Shelf has never recognized it. Spectra moves the date every few years, and has twice reported that the Marsh was evacuated without loss. The old people in Karasu keep the right date, and the city follows them instead of the screens.
