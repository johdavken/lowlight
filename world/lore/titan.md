Titan began in ore extraction and staked its future on asteroid mining. When space flight was largely abandoned in the 2050s because of fuel costs, that bet failed, and Titan has been declining ever since. It is still a big name. It remains a major importer, its old wealth is enormous, and it holds land, debt and grudges all over the city. Its Nexus offices are far too big for the staff it has left. Titan's people talk about orbit the way old soldiers talk about a war, and a failing corp with that much money and nothing to lose makes everyone nervous.
