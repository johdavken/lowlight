The Sump-Rats are the boat crews of the Marsh. They know every flooded street, collapsed floor and underwater passage, and they claim salvage on anything the water gives up: old machines, lost cargo, bodies. They run ferries across the Marsh for a fee, smuggle goods in through canals no patrol will follow, and charge outsiders to pass. Members mark their boats and their skin with tide lines. They are poor, tough and loyal to each other, and they hate the corps that abandoned the Marsh. Most of them are not cruel, but they are territorial, and people who try to work the water without them tend to be found in it. They trade with the Drowned Choir but don't trust it.
