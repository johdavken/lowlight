Clean Slate is Spectra's biggest show. Every week, houses put forward a handful of workers in debt, and the city watches them compete to have it wiped on air: run on carts they've never used, answer for their families, tell the worst thing they ever did. Viewers vote with paid calls. One wins and the rest go back to their houses with the cost of the broadcast added to what they owe. Nobody admits to watching, and every bar on the Ribbon has it on. Houses are paid for each worker they send, and some workers ask to go. Spectra says its winners are living well. Dead Air once played footage of a winner back on a house contract six months later, and the next week the show opened on a new set with a bigger prize. True-North has been looking for another winner for years.
