Along the outside edge of the Spine's deck, a narrow steel maintenance catwalk runs between the pylons, meant for crews who stopped coming years ago. Near Pylon Forty you can reach it through a service hatch off the top ring, or by climbing out past the hung habs, and both are trespass. The traffic roars past on the other side of the crash barrier, close enough to shake the grating, and on the open side there is only a waist-high rail and the whole city below: the core's towers, the Ribbon stacked up in light, the bay and its ship lights, the highway curving away on its legs. A sodium lamp leans out over the edge and a yellow warning beacon nobody switches off turns slowly at the end. Spiners go out there to smoke, to think, to be alone, or to be alone with someone. Nobody official ever comes, and nobody falls, mostly.
