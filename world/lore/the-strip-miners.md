The Strip-Miners are Lowlight's mercenary runners. They strip lacquer for anyone who pays: corps hitting other corps, fixers, gangs, people trying to erase a debt. They work in crews, with each runner specializing in one layer of defense, and they split every take by contract. They have no politics and say so; a Strip-Miner who worked for Cortex last week may hit Cortex this week. Their reputation rests on two things: they finish the job, and they never talk about it afterward. They meet in the dark and take most of their work through the Switchboard. The Capers hunt them more than any other clan, and every Strip-Miner knows someone who was caught and never seen again.
