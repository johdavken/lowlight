A pocket deck has no keyboard, only a thumb pad and a strip of screen, and it is small enough to tape inside a jacket or under a sleeve. It holds one or two carts and runs slow, and no runner would choose it for real work on the grid. It is the deck for walking into places: guards check bags and cuffs, not seams, and a pocket deck can open a door, wipe a camera loop, or pass a file in the time it takes someone to look away. Runners on a slip carry one. Owning one is not illegal; owning one with a door program on it is.
