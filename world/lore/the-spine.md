The Spine is a superhighway raised on enormous pylons. It cuts through the center of Lowlight, from Iron Reach past Nexus to Karasu and down toward the Marsh. It was built in the late 1900s, when the sea was taking ground and the city had to stack its roads to save space. It stands taller than nearly everything outside Helios Towers and Nexus. It counts as a district because people live in it: habs are built into the pylons and hung beneath the decks, whole neighborhoods in the dark under the road. Above them the traffic never stops. Rents are low and so is the ceiling. Residents call themselves Spiners and say they can tell the time by the sound of the freight.
