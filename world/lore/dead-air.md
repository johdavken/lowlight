Dead Air is a network of pirate broadcasters who cut into Spectra's feeds and replace them with the truth, or their version of it. A screen on the Ribbon flickers, the Spectra anchor vanishes, and for a minute or two Dead Air plays footage the city was never meant to see, often supplied by True-North. Then the feed snaps back and Spectra pretends nothing happened. Dead Air also runs illegal radio stations with music, news and call-in shows, and the stations move every few nights. Its members are a mix of activists, artists and runners, and nobody knows the full roster. Spectra and SDG hunt them constantly. Ordinary people love them and quote them, and would never admit it on the grid.
