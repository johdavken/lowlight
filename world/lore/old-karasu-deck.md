Old Karasu decks were built before the corps lacquered the grid: wooden or brass cases, valve-warm amber text, keyboards that clack like typewriters. They are slow, they hold almost nothing, and they speak protocols the modern grid was built on and the lacquer never quite closed. A runner on one can find ghost doors, ways into corp systems that no modern deck can see because nothing modern knows to look. Runners say there are three left in the city, and nobody agrees on who has them. Every few years someone claims to own one, and every few years someone stops talking about it.
