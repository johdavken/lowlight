Skarnholm is a cold freeport across the water, kept dry by dykes and lit by the wind farms along its coast. It is rich, orderly and closed, and the people who leave it are rarely poor. More often they are running from a debt, a contract or a corp they signed their life to. Skarnholmers who come through the Ferrymen tend to arrive with a corp sponsor's name on their papers and a good reason not to use it. In Lowlight they are quiet, precise and hard to read. Corps hire them as engineers and managers, and the Houses value them as staff who never make a scene. They find Lowlight loud and warm, and most never go back.
