The Freight Runners are Dockside's smugglers and thieves. They work the gaps in Voltaic's automation: the minutes between a crane setting a container down and a scanner reading it, the stacks the cameras can't see, the manifests a friendly runner can edit. Many are dock workers, or the children of dock workers Voltaic laid off, and they think of theft as back pay. They move contraband into the city, lift cargo out of it, and rent container space to anyone who needs to hide something, or someone. Voltaic tolerates them up to a point. The Freight Runners know exactly where that point is and stay just under it, and they deal with anyone who pushes past it themselves, before Aegis has to.
