Nobody teaches Lowlight's manners and everyone gets corrected. You don't ask where chrome came from, or what a cart is doing in someone's hands. You don't ask which house a person is with unless they say it first. You take the name you're given. You buy something if you sit, and you don't settle business in someone else's room. You don't say a number out loud, whether it's a debt, a price or a date. Thanks are kept short, because a favor is a debt and everyone can count. Nobody looks at the Q for long, and nobody films a stranger on a bad night. Upstack, people look past you. Downstack they look at your hands.
