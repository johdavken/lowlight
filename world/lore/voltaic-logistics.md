Voltaic Logistics moves everything in Lowlight that is not a person, and some things that are. It owns the automated shipping fleets in the bay, the sky-freighters overhead, the magnetic hyper-trains that carry freight through the city, and most of the cranes in Dockside and Iron Reach. If a crane is working, it is Voltaic's. Voltaic replaced most of its human workers years ago and keeps the rest on short contracts. Everything that crosses the city passes through Voltaic's systems at some point, which makes its manifests worth a great deal to runners.
