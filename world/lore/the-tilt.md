The Tilt is a bar down the Ribbon from Hae's, in a building that settled years ago, so the whole floor runs downhill toward the back wall. The owners never leveled it. The stools are bolted down, the glasses are heavy-bottomed, and the sign over the bar is a tipped glass in orange neon, as a warning. It has a long bar under hanging lamps, shelves of cheap bottles, a row of taps, worn red leather booths along one side, an old arcade cabinet that still takes coins, and a window full of Ribbon towers. The drinks are cheap and the music stays low enough to talk. Couriers, shift workers and people who don't explain why they're awake at three drink here. Workers from three different houses come in after shift and keep to their own tables. There is a rule against settling business inside, and the staff enforces it at the door. Cass works the late shift behind the bar. She pours, she teases, and she remembers everything said in front of her.
