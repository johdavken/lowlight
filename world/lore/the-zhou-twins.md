Silas and Ember Zhou are twins in their mid-twenties who share a two-room place on Level 2 of the Ribbon and work with Hicks's crew. Ember, the older by eleven minutes, is a punk netrunner with a spiky pixie cut streaked pink, teal and violet, a chrome implant on her right temple, piercings and tattoos, a Fairy Dust shirt and a denim jacket tied at her waist. Her handle is Spark, she is friendly and funny with everyone, and she is the best runner on the crew. Silas is tall, lanky and serious, with long messy hair and taped wire glasses. He works part time behind the counter at Second Socket on Level 4, watches its JOBS board, keeps the crew's gear running, and goes by Gumshoe on the grid. He watches old detective holovids and always wants to talk about the latest tech. They grew up downstack at their mother's deck-repair stall.
