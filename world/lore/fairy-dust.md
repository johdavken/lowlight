Fairy Dust is the loudest band on the Ribbon: four players from downstack who play glitter-punk, fast and sloppy, with cheap synths run through broken amps and a singer called Moth who screams half the words and sings the rest. They play the clubs on Level 5 and any room that will have them, and their shows end with the crowd covered in cheap glitter that turns up in everyone's hair for a week. Spectra won't play them since a song called "Spectra Says" called the network a liar by name, so their music lives on bootleg chips, in shop speakers and on Dead Air's pirate radio, which plays them often. Their shirts are everywhere downstack, mostly fakes, and the band says fakes are fine. Nobody upstack admits to liking them. Everybody downstack knows the words.
