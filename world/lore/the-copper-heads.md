The Copper-Heads are old-school runners who work through the city's original copper phone and data lines, laid in Karasu more than a century ago and forgotten by everyone else. Their gear is analog and handmade: patched decks, line taps, switch boxes hidden in Old Town walls. The copper network barely registers on the modern grid, so corp security almost never sees it, and the Copper-Heads use it to move messages, money and data where nobody can watch. Many of them live among the Hollows. They tend to be older than other runners, suspicious of new tech and contemptuous of carts. They charge more than anyone to move a message, and they lose fewer.
