Sundarpur is a vast monsoon delta city across the water, crowded, humid and half flooded every rainy season. It has more back-street clinics than anywhere else on the sea, and its cut-price chrome work and copied MiraGen script travel everywhere, which is why Lowlight's customs check Sundarpuri arrivals for medical papers more often than most. Sundarpuris come to Lowlight as clinic hands, technicians, nurses and cooks, often with real skills and no paperwork that proves them. Many end up working for the co-ops or the street clinics, where nobody asks for a licence. They are quick, practical and patient with bureaucracy, having grown up with plenty of it.
