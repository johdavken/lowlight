<p align="center"><b>Lowlight for SillyTavern: a roleplaying cyberpunk city, in your chat.</b></p>

**Lowlight** is a rain-soaked port city of wet streets, neon, and people with lives of their own. This
extension brings its world into SillyTavern:

- **The live rooms** behind your chat: lights, glow, rain on the glass, steam, passing cars, and lightning.
- **The cast:** characters and personas, each with their own sprites, and the city's lore book.
- **The city map.**
- **The rooms' songs.**
- **Lowlight's look.**

Your contact can change the room as you talk: the mood, the weather, the lights, or walking you both somewhere else.
And her **Residual** remembers: after each of her replies it keeps her notes on you and on herself, and a recap of
the chat so far, so a long chat doesn't forget what mattered.

## What's in it

**15 characters.** Ten you can talk to or play as, each also a persona: Nix, Cass, Ember, Silas, Hicks, Ines,
Mother Rot, Soren, Adebayo and Rue. Five more to talk to: Hae, Tilly, Vesna, Garro and Oksana.

**34 rooms,** district by district:

**The city map:** Lowlight rendered at night, every district named, with GO to walk you both there.

**The lore book:** 93 entries on the city, its districts and its people.

## The full game

This extension is the city and its people. **[Lowlight](https://github.com/johdavken/lowlight)** itself
adds the rest: missions, jobs, quests, a crew and the city's clock. Download the builds
for Windows and Linux from [itch.io](https://lowlightgame.itch.io/lowlight) or
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/johdavken/lowlight/releases), or run it from its open-source repository.

### What's only in the game

In SillyTavern, her reply and her Residual's quiet call are the only model calls, and SillyTavern's own chat, swipes and branches are the save.
Everything below lives in the game and isn't in this extension. More of the game's features will come over if there's enough interest.

**Memory and the Residual**
- **The rest of the Residual:** here it keeps her notes, a recap and the lights she forgot (see [Her Residual](#her-residual)), but not her dossier on you, and it doesn't move the clock.
- **Cipher and LOOSE ENDS:** no hidden truths, no clues, no record of what you've noticed.
- **Standing and romance:** no trust or warmth, no wants or kept secrets, and no romance gates. She feels about you however the chat says.

**Time and the city**
- **The story clock:** the clock in the Keys bar is your computer's time. There are no days, weekdays, WAIT, or nights that end.
- **Schedules and plans:** people don't keep hours or go anywhere on their own. There's no LET THEM GO / GO WITH THEM card, and AROUND LOWLIGHT doesn't list who's where.
- **Venue hours and story moods:** rooms don't change with the hour, and a closed place isn't closed.
- **City weather:** her weather changes only the room you're in. It doesn't follow you to the next one.
- **Blackouts:** the power never goes, so rooms, the HUD and the map never go dark with it.
- **Doors you click:** she can take you both through a room's ways out, but there are no door boxes to click. The map only goes to places whose room is in the package.

**People**
- **Fixed characters on stage:** Hae, Tilly, Vesna, Garro and Oksana are cards you can talk to, but they don't stand in their own places when you bring someone in, and your contact doesn't play them in the same reply.
- **Leads:** no give_lead and no LEADS tab.
- **The deck:** no texting, no promised texts, and no photos.
- **Comms:** no Chair, and no working ON COMMS from her desk.
- **Leaving and going solo:** no END SCENE, LEAVE or WALK OUT, and no /solo. Start another chat instead.
- **Strangers' faces:** quest and job strangers have no busts.
- **Faces from actions:** her face comes from SillyTavern's own Character Expressions extension, if you use it, not from what she does in *actions*.
- **Outfits:** each person wears their one outfit from the card. There's no wardrobe, and no WEARS line for a change of clothes.
- **Playthrough identity:** no per-playthrough identity overrides.

**Play**
- **Quests, jobs and big jobs:** no quest cards, boards, fixers, the intake board, odds, EDGE, sol, heat or district rep.
- **Gigs:** no Papers, Grid or Slip.
- **Crew:** no hiring, errands or roster.
- **The YOU sheet:** no stats or SHEET. YOU opens SillyTavern's persona panel, and PEOPLE opens its character list.
- **ENCOUNTER:** no narrator drawing three events. SillyTavern's own impersonate and narrator tools are yours to use.
- **SUGGEST:** SillyTavern's Impersonate does the same job.
- **The GO screen:** GO on the map walks you there, but there's no trip screen with who comes along or a line on the way.
- **Liar's dice** at the Low Roller.
- **Saves:** no LOAD, CONTINUE or BRANCH HERE. SillyTavern's chat list, swipes and branches do the job.

**Menus and sound**
- **The opening card, splash, title and district load screens.**
- **Some sounds:** the package carries only the songs and the CC0 sounds, so some of the game's room sounds and menu cues are missing or silent.
- **Bug reports:** the game's in-app report doesn't come along. Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/johdavken/lowlight/issues).

## Install

1. In SillyTavern, open **Extensions → Install extension**.
2. Paste `https://github.com/johdavken/lowlight-sillytavern` and install.
3. Reload. Lowlight asks once whether to install its cast (characters, personas and the lore book). Say **Y**.

To update, use **Extensions → Manage extensions → Update**. New characters install by themselves.

## Her Residual

After each of her replies, a second, quiet call reads what just happened and keeps her ledger:

- **Notes:** what she learns about you and what's going on in her own life, each with a few keywords. Pinned notes are always in front of her; the rest come back when their words come up in the chat.
- **A recap** of where things stand, every 10th reply, so a long chat keeps its older events.
- **Lights:** if she says the lamp went off but didn't turn it off, it does.

It's saved with each of her messages, so deleting, swiping or branching the chat takes the ledger back with it. A new
chat starts a new ledger. Read it with **SYS → RESIDUAL → LEDGER**, or type `/ledger` (on a phone too).

**It costs one more call per reply**, on the same connection and model as the chat. Switch it off in SYS → RESIDUAL, or
with **O** in `/ledger`. A model that thinks before it answers costs more here, and one that runs out of room before
answering just leaves that reply unread.

## Settings it needs

- **API:** Chat Completion → OpenRouter, with a model that supports tool calling. GLM (`z-ai/glm-5.2`) is what Lowlight is tuned on. On a Text Completion backend the rooms, sound and cast all work, but your contact can't change the room.
- **Enable function calling** (AI Response Configuration). The welcome switches it on for you. Without it the rooms still render, but your contact can't change them.
- **Context size of 16k tokens or more; 32k is better** (same panel). SillyTavern's default of 4,095 leaves no room for the conversation once the card and lore are in, so your contact would answer with no memory of the chat. If yours is still at that default, the welcome raises it to 32k; a size you set yourself is left alone. Both settings are SillyTavern's own, so they apply to all your chats.

## A first scene

1. Type `/select`. Pick who you play (say, Hicks) and who you call (Nix). The chat opens in her room, the
   Lowlight Intake, with rain on the window behind it.
   Each persona keeps its own chat with her: come back as Hicks and you're back in his.
2. Talk. *"Kill the lights, someone's watching the street."* She dims the room herself.
3. *"Let's get noodles."* She walks you both to the Noodle Stall: the room changes behind the chat, and so does the sound.
4. Or open the map with **Alt+M**, pick a place, and press GO.

Without tool calling she can't do steps 2 and 3, but you can: `/mood` and `/room` change the room yourself.

## Keys

- **Alt+M** opens the city map; GO walks you both there.
- **Alt+Y** opens your persona, **Alt+P** the characters.
- **SYS** (top right): sound levels, every SillyTavern panel, CAST, HER ROOM TOOLS, and LOOK.
- `/ledger` opens her Residual's ledger for this chat, and its ON/OFF switch.
- `/select` opens the character select, `/room <name>` and `/mood <name>` change the room, and `/lowchat` switches between Lowlight's look and SillyTavern's own.
