Shrikes are SDG's counter-intelligence, the people who watch SDG. A shrike is a small grey bird that kills things its own size and hangs them on thorns where the others can see. The office has no floor of its own and nobody admits to working in it. A shrike is the colleague who asks how your week was. They read the branch's files, the case officers' expenses, the hours of glass footage nobody else has time to watch, and the accounts of Sentinels who suddenly stop needing overtime. They don't fire anyone. An officer who fails a review is reassigned, then reassigned again, then let go with a file that closes every door in Lowlight except Aegis. That is why so many Aegis officers are ex-SDG, and why none of them will say why they left. Outside SDG almost nobody knows the word. Inside, nobody says it above a whisper, and everyone assumes there is one at the next desk.
