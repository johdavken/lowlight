Kalimera Bay is a sunbaked harbour town across the water, old white stone stacked up a hillside above a bay that keeps getting higher. The sea has already taken its lower streets, the way it took the Marsh, and the fishing that kept it alive has thinned to almost nothing. Its people leave by boat, whole families at once, and arrive on Tidal Isle with a little money, a lot of relatives and very few papers. Kalimerans in Lowlight run small kitchens, mend boats and nets, and drift toward the Marsh and the Strand, anywhere near water. They are warm, loud and slow to trust officials of any kind. The name means good morning, and they still say it to each other at any hour.
