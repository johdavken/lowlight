The Cortex Meridian is the deck the corps issue and the one ordinary people can buy legally: a beige slab with a good keyboard, fast, reliable, and licensed. Like a cart, a Meridian checks in over the grid. Cortex can update it, throttle it, or switch it off, and it knows where every one of them is. Runners call it the leash. A corp runner on a Meridian is the fastest thing on the grid, and the easiest to find. Street runners won't touch a clean one, and a runner who shows up with one is either new, stupid, or working for someone they shouldn't be.
