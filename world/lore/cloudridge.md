Cloudridge sits in the hills on Lowlight's north side. Its mansions line winding roads between the ridges, behind walls, gates and some of the last real trees in the city. It is old money: families who were rich before the corps and stayed rich by marrying into them, investing in them or holding their debts. Cloudridge looks down on the whole city. On clear nights the Velvet cat is plain to see from its terraces, and old Cloudridge families pretend not to notice it. Private security patrols the roads, and anyone on foot is stopped. The newer corporate rich live east in Helios Towers, and Cloudridge looks down on them too.
