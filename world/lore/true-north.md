True-North is a clan of ghosts devoted to the truth. They copy Spectra broadcasts, corporate filings and city records before those can be rewritten, and they keep the originals in hidden archives scattered across the grid and on paper. They leak what they find, often through Dead Air, and they never sell it. Anyone can try to reach them to ask for the real version of an event, but True-North decides who gets an answer. Nobody knows how many of them there are. Spectra calls them terrorists and pays the Capers to find them. To many people in Lowlight, True-North is the only reason anyone remembers what actually happened.
