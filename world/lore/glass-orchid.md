A companion house in Velvet, Lowlight's red-light quarter: discreet and expensive. A house places its workers and holds their payout, which is how it keeps them. Nix Neon worked out of Glass Orchid for nine years before she left. Leaving is possible, and the house remembers. Its Host is Vesna Halloran, who keeps the floor all night at the long bar under the paper lanterns. Whether she owns the house or fronts for someone who does is anyone's guess.
