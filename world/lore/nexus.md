Nexus is Lowlight's core. The most powerful corps keep their headquarters here, and this is where the city is actually run. Its towers are the brightest in Lowlight, wrapped in moving signs and logos, and the streets between them are clean, patrolled and full of people walking fast. Cortex Global, Sentinel Defense Group, Soma, MiraGen, Chiron, Voltaic, Titan and Spectra all have their seats here, alongside dozens of smaller corps hoping to join them. The banks that back the sol are here too. The Shelf passes the laws, but Nexus drafts them. Ordinary people come to Nexus to work, apply, appeal or be judged, and they leave as soon as they can.
