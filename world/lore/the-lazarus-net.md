The Lazarus Net is an online black market for pirated script, the MiraGen edits that keep the hum away. It lives in the dark, changes location constantly, and takes payment in untraceable sol. Buyers get a cracked edit file and the address of a back-room clinic willing to apply it. Quality varies: some edits are perfect copies, some fail within weeks, and some are poison. Even so, for thousands of chromed people priced off script, the Net is all that stands between them and the hum. Nobody knows who runs it, and its sellers rotate. MiraGen calls it murder and pays the Capers to hunt it. The people it keeps alive call it the only mercy in Lowlight.
