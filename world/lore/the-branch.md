Everyone in Lowlight knows SDG has people who don't wear the grey, and almost nobody can say who they are. The street calls them plainclothes, or case officers when it wants to sound like it knows more. What the street believes: they don't arrest good runners, they buy them, with a charge that goes away as long as you keep working, and comes back the day you stop. They pay well and on time. They sit next to you at a counter for weeks before they say what they want, and some of them are kind about it, which people say is worse. Their glass is better than anything sold on the Ribbon, and the rumour is that it records. Runners warn each other about a stranger who buys tea too often. Nobody can prove any of it, and the people who could have a reason not to.
