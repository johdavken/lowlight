Lowlight was founded in 1878 as Karasu, a harbor settlement on the bay. The old name survives in its oldest district. The city grew outward from the harbor in rings of brick, then concrete, then glass. In the late 1900s the sea started coming in. The city raised the Spine to carry its roads over the water and save the ground that was left, and from then on it built up instead of out. The Marsh went under anyway. In the 2050s fuel costs ended most space flight. The companies that had bet on orbit, Titan above all, came home poorer and meaner. The age of the corps followed. Nexus became the real seat of power, and the Shelf became a place where laws are signed rather than made. Lowlight does not teach its own history. Spectra changes the story every few years. The oldest people in Karasu still tell it the way it happened.
