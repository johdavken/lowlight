The Ferrymen get people into Lowlight through Tidal Isle. They forge papers, bribe customs officers, hide people in cargo, and run them off the Isle by boat at night. Their price is high and few can pay it up front, so most clients pay in labor, owed for years to a house the Ferrymen choose. Some never finish paying. The Ferrymen also get people out of the holding blocks, and sometimes out of Lowlight entirely, for anyone with enough sol. They have officers inside customs and boats on the bay, and they work with the Freight Runners and the Sump-Rats. They are polite and punctual, and they show no mercy at all to anyone who tries to skip a debt.
