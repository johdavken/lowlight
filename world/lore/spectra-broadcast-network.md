Spectra Broadcast Network, SBN or just Spectra, is Lowlight's news conglomerate. It owns most of the channels, feeds and screens in the city, and every day it tells Lowlight what happened, in whatever version the highest-paying corporate board wants. Spectra does more than spin the news. It rewrites the past: events change, people are added or removed, and old broadcasts are quietly replaced. Spectra says means probably false. People who want the truth ask the old people, check paper, or pay a runner to find the original files before Spectra overwrites them.
