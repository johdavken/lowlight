A booth deck is rented by the hour in a back booth, a curtain and a stool and a deck chained to the table. Second Socket has a row of them; so do half the bars on the Ribbon. It is how people with no deck of their own get on the grid: a kid paying off a debt, a runner whose own deck just died, somebody who needs one job done and no record of owning anything. The catch is that the booth's owner logs every keystroke, and sells the logs to whoever asks nicely. Experienced runners use a booth only for work they don't mind being sold.
